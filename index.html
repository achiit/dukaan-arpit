A neighbourhood clinic, run like family

I started this clinic in 2014 after watching my uncle travel to Delhi every week just for physiotherapy. He was 68, and each trip left him more tired than the treatment helped. I wanted people in Lal Kuan to get good care without that journey.

Today many of our patients come from the same lanes I grew up in. I still remember most of them by name, and by their knees and shoulders.

We explain what is wrong in plain words, give only the treatment you need, and tell you the fee before we begin.
